# PART A — Plain English Walkthrough (for your wife / the team)

## What this app is
A custom-built AI-powered music management studio designed for an indie label run by a couple. It lets you:
- Build out fictional / AI artists with full brand identity and reference photos
- Track every song you create — your originals, your AI-collabs, and pure-AI experiments
- Generate Suno-ready style prompts and Sora/Runway-ready video storyboards
- Brainstorm with a Creative Assistant that knows your whole catalog and your writing voice
- Plan EPs / albums / singles, track distribution, and watch revenue roll in
- Work as a **team** — share the entire catalog with each other while still keeping personal/private songs

## How to log in (first time)

You can either use the existing demo account or **create your own**.

### Option 1 — Use your own email (recommended)
1. Open the app → tap **"Sign Up"** at the bottom of the login screen
2. Enter your name, your email, and a password
3. Hit **"Create Account"** — you'll land on the dashboard immediately, in your own private workspace.

### Option 2 — Use the shared demo accounts
- **Music Exec (your husband's account):** `exec@music.com` / `password123`
- **Test/Wife account:** `test@example.com` / `password123`

## How to join your husband's catalog (Team Workspace)

This is the magic part: instead of two separate apps, you both work on the same catalog.

1. **He generates the code:**
   - On the dashboard, top-right → tap the 🧑 **person-circle icon**
   - Tap **"Generate Invite Code"**
   - A 6-character code appears (e.g., `K3X9PA`) — valid for 7 days. He copies it and texts it to you.

2. **You join the team:**
   - Sign into your own account
   - Top-right → person-circle → scroll to **"Join a Team"**
   - Type the 6-character code → tap **"Join Team"**
   - Done! You instantly see all his artists, songs, collections, and ideas. You can edit them, leave comments, save AI prompts, anything.

3. **Want to keep something private?**
   - Anywhere you create or edit an artist, song, collection, or idea, there's an **"is_private"** flag — set it to true and only you'll see it.
   - You can also **leave the team** anytime (red button on the Team screen) to fall back to your own personal workspace.

## The 6 main tabs

### 1. 🏠 Home (Dashboard)
- Counters at top: total Artists, Songs, Ideas
- Song Status breakdown (Draft / In Progress / Final / Released)
- Revenue chart with total earnings
- Recent songs feed
- **Top right:** 💬 chat-bubble icon (Creative Assistant) and 🧑 person-circle (Team & Profile)

### 2. 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Artists
- Searchable / filterable roster of every artist in the catalog
- Tap **+** to create a new artist with: name, bio, unique sound, themes, tone, branding (color palette, mood keywords, aesthetic, visual style), Suno voice ID, and exclusion prompts
- Tap any artist to open their profile, where you'll find:
  - **Profile image** (uploaded base64 or external URL)
  - **🆕 Character Gallery** — multiple reference photos (different outfits, angles, moods, locations) for music videos. Upload from your phone or paste image URLs.
  - **Visual Brief** — auto-generated, shareable visual identity description
  - **Suno voice + exclusions** for consistent generation

### 3. 🎵 Songs
- The catalog. Search bar, status filters, sort options.
- Three buttons in the header:
  - **✨ Quick AI** (NEW — purple) → paste lyrics, AI auto-fills genre/mood/themes/styles
  - **☁️ CSV Import** → bulk import from a Google Sheet or spreadsheet (tab-delimited)
  - **+ Add** → manual full form
- Tap any song → opens a packed detail screen:
  - Title, primary artist, featured artists, collection, status, track number
  - **Authorship** — original / AI-collab / AI-generated (helps train the assistant on your real voice)
  - **Lyrics** with copy button
  - **3 style fields** — primary, secondary, alternative (for A/B testing on Suno)
  - **Style exclusions**
  - **Suno generations list** — every generated track with its URL, prompt, rating, and notes
  - **Versions** — demo, final cut, TikTok edit, etc.
  - **Comments** — collaborative thread between you two
  - **🆕 AI Prompts Gallery** — every AI-generated prompt you save (Suno styles, video storyboards, Assistant chat outputs) lives here, copy-pasteable. There's an **"✨ Assistant"** button that takes you straight to chat with this song pre-loaded as context.
  - **Action buttons** — Share (platform-specific copy) and Distribution (where the song is posted)

### 4. 💿 Releases (Collections)
- Albums, EPs, LPs, Singles, Playlists, Projects
- Cover art, release date, status, description
- **Tracklist with up/down reorder** — drag tracks into the right order
- Auto track-count

### 5. 💡 Ideas
- Brainstorming board — sparks, concepts, lyric snippets, melody ideas, style notes, visual concepts
- Tag and link to specific artists/songs

### 6. 🤖 AI Tools
- **Creative Assistant CTA** at the top (purple) → opens the chat
- **Save outputs below to:** picker — pick which song the next generated prompt should attach to
- Three AI tools below:
  - **Lyric/theme analyzer** — paste lyrics, get themes, mood, style suggestions
  - **Suno Prompt Generator** — fill genre/mood/key/tempo + (optional) artist context, get a copy-paste-ready Suno style block. Click **"Save to Song"** to drop into that song's gallery.
  - **Video Storyboard Generator** — outputs Sora/Runway parameters + a 60-second TikTok cut script. Also savable to song.

## The 🆕 Creative Assistant (Chat)

Open it from:
- 💬 chat-bubble icon on the dashboard
- "Open Creative Assistant" CTA on the AI tab
- "✨ Assistant" button on any song's profile (auto-links that song)

What it does:
- Multi-turn conversation that remembers everything in your catalog
- Suggested prompts to start: "Help me brainstorm a new artist concept", "Write lyrics in my voice for a song about heartbreak", "Suggest visual branding for my latest artist", "Plan a release strategy for my upcoming EP", etc.
- **Context bar** at the top — link an artist or song to give the AI deeper context for its replies
- Every assistant message has **Copy** and (when a song is linked) **Save to Song** buttons — drops the message straight into that song's prompt gallery
- Tap the 🔄 refresh icon to start a brand-new conversation

## Workflows you'll actually use

### "I just wrote a chorus on my phone — get it into the system fast"
1. Songs tab → tap **✨ Quick AI**
2. Paste the lyrics
3. AI fills in genre, mood, themes, primary/secondary/alternative styles
4. Hit save → song lives in the catalog

### "Generate me 3 Suno prompts for our new artist's vibe"
1. AI Tools tab
2. Pick the song from the "Save outputs below to:" picker
3. Use Suno Prompt Generator → tap "Save to Song"
4. Open the song → AI Prompts Gallery has it ready to copy/paste into Suno

### "I want to brainstorm with the AI about a new artist concept"
1. Tap 💬 from dashboard
2. Type or pick a suggested prompt
3. Iterate — "make her edgier", "give me 3 more outfit ideas", etc.
4. Save the bits you like to a song's gallery

### "My wife and I both want to work on the catalog tonight"
- He generates an invite code on the Team screen → texts it to her → she joins → both edit live, both leave comments. Each can mark personal songs as private.

## Team-Aware Query Helper

All list/single GETs go through `team_query()` in `/app/backend/server.py`:
```python
def team_query(current_user, base=None):
    q = dict(base or {})
    q["team_id"] = current_user.team_id
    privacy_clause = {"$or": [
        {"user_id": current_user.id},
        {"is_private": {"$ne": True}},
    ]}
    # merges privacy_clause with any existing $or/$and
    return q
```
On startup, a one-time migration pipeline backfills `team_id = user_id` on any pre-existing record.
